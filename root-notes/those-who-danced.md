# Those Who Danced
### The Second Sun Altar

---

“And there were those who danced in the light of the second sun. Remember us.”

— original line from a poem by Jacobo Hernández Durbin, written in Hiroshima, Japan, 1998

---

This altar holds:

ash-light,  
stone-memory,  
curved forms,  
seed and bone,  
the window,  
the garden beyond the screen,  
and the old instruction:

remember us.

---

The shadow is part of the altar.  
It arrives with flame.

The shadow does not match the form.

At night,  
something stands up on the wall.
